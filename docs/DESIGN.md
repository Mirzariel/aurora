# AURORA design direction

## Approved rules

- A quiet, cinematic six-chapter story in navy, blue and green, inspired by a flat layered aurora curtain.
- One desktop sticky packet journey, driven by native scrolling; mobile is linear. Hardware, dashboard, evidence and team use normal scrolling.
- Respect reduced motion and save-data; content remains readable with JavaScript disabled. Keep the site local, static and buildless.
- Distinguish design, simulation, tests and unverified capabilities; preserve evidence and limitations.

## Implementation choices

- Geist is the main typeface for headings and narrative, with Geist Mono reserved for chapter numbers, specifications and evidence labels. No third font is introduced. Broad editorial spacing, restrained borders and a light hardware/evidence interlude establish hierarchy.
- The packet follows scroll in both directions; all five explanations remain visible. Narrow or short screens use the linear fallback.
- The re-rendered old PCB model and simulated dashboard link to full-size images. Further PCB views are available through an accessible native disclosure. The hero plate is a generative illustration, clearly labelled on desktop and mobile.
- No unfinished 3D viewer or empty video placeholder is activated.
- The Simalungun PJU-TS photograph is contextual and is credited at the image; it is not an AURORA trial. It was resized and reformatted under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- A short stock night-rain clip bridges context and the packet journey. It is poster-first, manually played through native controls, and does not download the video until playback. Its lamps are not established as solar powered.
- Sticky enhancement is enabled only when the actual content fits the viewport; smaller, shorter, reduced-motion and save-data layouts remain linear.
